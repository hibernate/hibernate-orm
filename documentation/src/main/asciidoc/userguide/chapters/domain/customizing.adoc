==== Mapping annotations and AttributeBinder
:example-dir-attributebinder: {core-project-dir}/src/test/java/org/hibernate/orm/test/mapping/attributebinder
:domain-customizing-extrasdir: extras

For mapping requirements beyond the built-in annotations, use the `@AttributeBinderType` meta-annotation
and the `AttributeBinder` contract to customize Hibernate's boot-time mapping model,
defined in the `org.hibernate.mapping` package.

====

Both `@YesNo` and `YesNoBinder` are application-defined. Hibernate processes `@YesNo`
through its `@AttributeBinderType` meta-annotation, which identifies `YesNoBinder` as the binder.

The binder receives the custom annotation instance, providing type-safe access to its members.
Although `@YesNo` has no members in this example, a custom annotation can define configuration
that its binder reads and applies.
